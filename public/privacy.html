Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Effective date: November 2, 2026

Welcome to Cricketle (https://playcricketle.onrender.com). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access our website and play our cricket trivia games.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver and personalize your gaming experience:

  • Google Account Information: If you sign in via Google One-Tap or Google Sign-In, we receive your Google ID, verified email address, full name, and profile picture provided by Google OAuth. We never access your Google password, contacts, or private Google Drive files.
  • Phone & Account Credentials: If you sign up using a mobile number, we store your username, mobile phone number, and a cryptographically hashed PIN. We never store plain-text passwords or PINs.
  • Gameplay Data: We store your game completions, solve times, guess counts, streaks, speed bonuses, and variant achievements to compute daily and all-time leaderboards.
  • Technical & Log Data: Like most web applications, our servers automatically log standard network information such as IP addresses, browser user-agents, and timestamps to enforce anti-cheat protections, rate limiting, and security audits.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and keep you securely logged into your game across sessions and devices.
  • To save your daily puzzle progress, streaks, achievements, and leaderboard rankings.
  • To protect Cricketle from bot spam, unauthorized access, and unfair score manipulation.
  • To maintain and improve game stability, server performance, and user experience.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

We only utilize trusted infrastructure providers to operate the service:

  • Google Identity Services: To facilitate quick, 1-tap Google authentication. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase: Encrypted cloud database hosting our player records, game statistics, and leaderboards under strict access controls.
  • Render: Cloud hosting infrastructure providing secure HTTPS transport and web servers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Cricketle uses secure, HTTP-only session cookies (cricketle_sid) and modern browser local storage exclusively for:

  • Maintaining your authenticated session.
  • Remembering your visual theme preference (Day / Night mode).
  • Caching today's puzzle state on your local device to enable instant load times.

We do not use tracking cookies for third-party behavioral advertising.

5. Data Security

We implement rigorous industry-standard security measures, including:

  • Strict Transport Security (HTTPS) across all web requests.
  • Cryptographic hashing of user credentials using secure algorithms.
  • Strict Content Security Policy (CSP), Cross-Origin protections, and rate limiting against automated attacks.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may at any time:

  • Request an export of your game profile and statistics.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account, Google identity link, and gameplay history.

To request deletion of your account and associated data, simply contact us at the email address below with your username or registered email.

7. Children's Privacy

Cricketle is a cricket trivia puzzle game intended for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent.
