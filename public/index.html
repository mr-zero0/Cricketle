          How to play

          A secret cricketer is picked for each puzzle. Guess any cricketer and you get a rank: 1 is the answer, and the lower the number, the closer you are.

          Closeness comes from nation, role, batting hand, bowling style, era, IPL teams, ICC titles and career numbers.

          Modes

          Easy picks from well-known players and shows tags for what each guess shares with the answer. Hard uses all players and shows only the rank.

          Three puzzles a day

          Each mode has three puzzles a day. They unlock one at a time, are the same for everyone who is signed in, and reset at midnight IST. A finished puzzle can be reviewed but not replayed.

          Hints and points

          Every puzzle has three hints: nation, then role, then era. Each costs 10 points.

          A solve is worth 100 points, minus 3 per guess after the first, 10 per hint and 1 per 20 seconds. Hard mode counts 1.5×. Giving up scores 0.
