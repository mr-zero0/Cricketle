      How to play

      A secret cricketer is picked each day. Guess any cricketer and you get a rank: 1 is the answer, and the lower the number, the closer you are.

      The colour of each guess follows its rank:

      The pool has players, every one of whom has played at least one Test, one ODI and one T20I.

      Closeness comes from nation, role, batting hand, bowling style, era, IPL teams, ICC titles and career numbers.

      Easy shows the rank, the colour and tags for what the guess shares with the answer. Hard shows only the rank and the colour.

      Five puzzles a day in each mode, ten different players in all. They unlock one at a time, are the same for everyone, and reset at midnight IST. A finished puzzle can be reviewed but not replayed.
